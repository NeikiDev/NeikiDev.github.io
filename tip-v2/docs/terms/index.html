Terms of Service

Welcome to the Threat Insights Portal. By accessing or using our online analysis tool, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms of service. Please read them carefully.

1. Service Description​

The Threat Insights Portal is an online tool for analyzing files to determine if they contain malware. Files submitted to the platform are processed using several third-party online analysis tools. Users are responsible for reviewing and agreeing to the policies of these third-party services.

2. File Submission and Retention​

  • Files submitted to the Threat Insights Portal are stored temporarily and will not be retained for longer than 30 days.
  • During the analysis process, metadata and content from the submitted files may be extracted for processing and improvement of our services.

3. User Accounts​

  • The platform uses a token-based login system. No passwords are stored.
  • We collect and store user profile information, including email addresses, names, and other relevant details, to provide and improve our services.

4. Data Protection and Security​

  • We use Cloudflare for DDoS protection, analytics, and IP address blocking to ensure the security and availability of our platform.

5. User Responsibilities​

  • Users must ensure that they have the right to upload and analyze the files submitted to the platform.

If you have any questions about these terms, please contact us at discord.
